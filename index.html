Private timelines, portable forever.

A local-first approach for creating simple life, travel, project, or career timelines. Easy to export and share -- you control your data!

Launch editor Open player

Local-first

Nothing stored online. Timelines live in your browser and are imported/exported to your files.

Open-source, open formats

Timelines are saved as a small, structured JSON format that anyone can read, edit, or build on. The code is open source on GitHub.

Shareable stand-alone playback

Export a single HTML file that plays your timeline as a flat timeline, a photo slideshow, or an interactive map* -- or write your own player.

Photos and places

Attach photos and locations to any event. The map player can even look up missing coordinates for you.*

Flexible by design

Embed photos in the file or link them from elsewhere. Sort events into categories with your own labels, and group them into collections. Add custom fields, to any event.

For any kind of story

Life milestones, trips, project histories, or a career path -- one simple format covers them all.

* The map player requires online access to load map tiles and look up places.
